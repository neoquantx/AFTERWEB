### Model Provenance & Weights
* **Model**: Qwen3 open-weight language model (`qwen3:4b` or `qwen3:1.7b`).
* **License**: Apache 2.0 (as distributed via Ollama).
* **Execution**: Local-only inference via loopback Ollama (`http://127.0.0.1:11434`). No remote LLM endpoint or telemetry is used.

### Archival Content & Data Attribution
* Archival metadata and capture citations are retrieved via the **Internet Archive Wayback Machine** (Wayback CDX Server API & web archives).
* Captures belong to their respective original publishers and are referenced under research, historical indexing, and fair informational reference practices.
* Missing capture queries are recorded as *"not found in checked query"* and do not assert that a page never existed or was permanently destroyed.
* Brief text excerpts are preserved solely for factual citation attribution (`[E#]`). Full historical pages should be viewed directly on the Internet Archive.
