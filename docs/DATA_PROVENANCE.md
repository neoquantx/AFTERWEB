# Data Provenance & Ingestion Policy

## Core Principle: Evidence Before Atmosphere

AFTERWEB adheres to strict evidence preservation:
* Every solid route in the 3D city is generated from an exact `<a href="...">` anchor observed in an archived HTML capture.
* Every citation `[E#]` links back to a stored chunk with original URL, Wayback URL, timestamp, and byte/char offset.
* Missing pages are represented as **Ghost Plots** (`not_found_in_query`) rather than 404 errors or AI hallucinations.

## Curated Seed Neighborhoods

   * **Archive Source**: Historical captures and Warner Bros. preservation repository.
   * **Significance**: Archetypal early commercial web promotion frozen in time.

   * **Significance**: High outward link density showcasing missing destinations.
